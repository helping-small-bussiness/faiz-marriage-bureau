Chapter One

Who We Are

Faiz Marriage Bureau was founded by Jamal Khan in Moradabad with one purpose: to help Muslim families find life partners with dignity, respect, and care — the way it's meant to be done, not through a directory, but through genuine guidance.

Every family that comes to us is treated with the same trust and privacy we'd want for our own — from the first conversation to the day of Nikah.
